# Bot Buddy Chrome/Edge Extension 🤖

> Direct browser assistant for Facebook engagement and 1-click backend session syncing.

## 🚀 How to Install in Chrome / Edge / Brave (Takes 15 Seconds):

1. Open your browser and go to:
   - **Chrome**: `chrome://extensions/`
   - **Edge**: `edge://extensions/`
   - **Brave**: `brave://extensions/`

2. In the top-right corner, turn ON **"Developer mode"** (ডানদিকের ওপরে Developer mode অন করুন).

3. Click the **"Load unpacked"** (লোড আনপ্যাকড) button in the top-left.

4. Select this folder from your PC:
   ```text
   D:\Projects\bot_buddy\extension
   ```

5. Done! The **Bot Buddy** icon will now appear in your browser's extension toolbar! 📌 Pin it for easy access.

---

## 🎯 What You Can Do With It:

1. **Direct 1-Click Like & Love Reaction**:
   - Open any Facebook post in your browser (e.g., `https://www.facebook.com/photo?fbid=...`).
   - Click the Bot Buddy extension icon.
   - Click **"👍 Like This Post (Direct)"** or **"❤️ Love React"**.
   - It clicks the real Facebook button right inside your browser!

2. **1-Click Smart Commenting**:
   - Select a positive/engaging comment preset and click **"💬 Post Smart Comment"**.
   - It types and submits the comment natively on your active Facebook tab.

3. **1-Click Session Cookie Sync**:
   - Click **"🔄 1-Click Sync Facebook to Backend"**.
   - It automatically extracts your logged-in Facebook session (`c_user` & `xs`) and sends it directly to your local Bot Buddy backend (`http://localhost:8000`), so you NEVER have to copy-paste cookies manually!
