## 🎯 Quick Overview
Bot Buddy is designed to simulate social media engagement metrics for testing, analytics development, and simulation purposes:
- **Dummy Followers & Page Likes** (with natural retention & drip-feed pacing)
- **Synthetic Likes & Reactions** (Standard Like, Love, Haha, Wow, Sad, Angry)
- **Dynamic Views & Retention Simulation** (Video views, Reels, Story views)
- **Smart Comments** (Spintax, custom templates, and AI-powered contextual comments)
- **Persona Engine** (Realistic avatars, names, locales, and human-like stochastic behavior)
- **Standard SMM API v2 & Modern Dashboard**
